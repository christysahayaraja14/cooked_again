How publishing works

Save Changes writes your menu and gallery to Supabase — every visitor sees the new dishes, prices and photos straight away. No files to upload.

Photo uploads are compressed in your browser and stored in your Supabase Storage bucket gallery. You can also paste any image link instead.

Orders, reservations & feedback arrive in the Supabase dashboard under Table Editor (orders, reservations, feedback) as well as by email.
